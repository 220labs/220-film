# Firsts, as far as we could find

220 makes one "first" claim: **it is the first documentary edited by the AI in it.** Claude Opus
5.5 chose every cut and wrote the cut list, the renderer and the credit roll; Claude agents are
one of the film's two protagonists; and the sessions that cut the film appear in its epilogue.
Claude is also credited with directing and producing, beside Ilya Fomin and Zhan Petrov; that is a
credit, not a "first".

We looked before saying so. On 2026-10-11 the filmmakers ran a prior-art search with Claude: six
research agents on Claude Sonnet 5.5 made 505 searches and page reads across festival listings (by
search), reviews, broadcaster pages, YouTube and GitHub, and Claude Fable 5.1 at its `xhigh` effort judged
the results and read the decisive pages itself. Nothing we found contradicts the claim. The
nearest earlier films are below, with what each one did.

**Know an earlier one? Open a pull request** that adds it to the table, with a link. If it holds,
the claim comes down and the table stays. (A merged pull request is copied into the film's working
repository before the next publish, so it is never undone.)

## What we do not claim

- **Not the first documentary with an AI protagonist.** *Deepfaking Sam Altman* (2025) has that
  word in print, and *I Hope This Helps!*, *More Human Than Human* and *About a Hero* each put an
  AI at the centre before us. In 220 the AI is one of two protagonists, not the only one.
- **Not the first documentary edited by an AI.** *This Documentary Was Made Entirely by AI*
  (2026-09-28) was cut in code by an AI eleven days before 220 premiered, and *Eno* is sometimes
  called AI-edited, which its makers reject.
- **Not the first film directed by an AI.** The film above was "written and directed by Fig"
  (2026-09-28), and in *Deepfaking Sam Altman* the chatbot "begins co-directing the movie" inside
  its story. Claude shares 220's director credit with two people.
- **Not the first open-source documentary.** *RiP!: A Remix Manifesto* (2008) claimed that in
  those words; Blender's *Elephants Dream* (2006) was "the world's first Open Movie" in BlenderNation's words.
- **Not the first film with a daily build.** Perry Bard's *Man with a Movie Camera: The Global
  Remake* (2007): "Every day a new version of the movie is built."
- **Not the first film about AI agents.** 60 Minutes ran a segment on Claudius, Anthropic's
  shop-running agent, in November 2025.

What 220 does, in one line: the AI in it is real, nothing in it was done for the camera, and the
AI that is in it cut it.
