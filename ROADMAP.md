# Roadmap

Steps, not dates. Each one is done when it is published here.

## v0.1

The recipe and the credits: the cut lists, the render scripts, the music prompts and
`CREDITS.yaml`. Public and tagged since 2026-10-10; its film goes up once the build uploads it,
which is not wired yet. v0.0 is the version that premiered on 2026-10-09.

## Next, unversioned

- The making-of and the rough cut lists, so the development of the film can be read.
- Review copies of the footage for a few trusted contributors, under a no-distribution licence.
  The repo itself carries no footage.
- The daily build from `main`. Every push to `main` renders the film and its teaser, and both go
  up on the 220 channel, unlisted. At 05:55 PT the day's version is cut: the latest build from
  `main` becomes the next minor version, its teaser goes public and its film premieres at
  11:11 PT. Anything that lands after 05:55 goes into the next day's version; a day with nothing
  new since the last version has no premiere. The release notes come from the commit summaries
  the version includes. Forks and other branches do not build.

## 1.0

A checklist, not a date. It is reached when all four are true:

- [ ] Every person's appearance consent is recorded.
- [ ] The dialogue is fixed.
- [ ] The music is chosen.
- [ ] The uncut edition is decided.
