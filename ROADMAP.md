# Roadmap

Steps, not dates. Each one is done when it is published here.

## v0.1

The recipe and the credits: the cut lists, the render scripts, the music prompts and
`CREDITS.yaml`. It is tagged at the first publish and premieres on Saturday 2026-10-10. v0.0 is
the version that premiered on 2026-10-09.

## Next, unversioned

- The making-of and the rough cut lists, so the development of the film can be read.
- Review copies of the footage for a few trusted contributors, under a no-distribution licence.
  The repo itself carries no footage.
- The daily build from `main`. On a day when `main` has new contributions, one teaser and one
  premiere go out on the 220 channel at 11:11 PT, at most. Each is one minor version. The release
  notes come from the commit summaries the version includes. Forks and other branches do not
  build.

## 1.0

A checklist, not a date. It is reached when all four are true:

- [ ] Every person's appearance consent is recorded.
- [ ] The dialogue is fixed.
- [ ] The music is chosen.
- [ ] The uncut edition is decided.
